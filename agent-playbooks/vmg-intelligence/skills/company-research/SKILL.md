# VMG Company Research

Use the existing VMG research engine/methodology; do not invent a parallel report framework.

## Process

1. Resolve the exact legal entity first; use supported identifiers/jurisdiction.
2. Select the appropriate existing template.
3. Research applicable areas: identity/registration, directors/promoters/ownership, related entities, business/products/plants/capacity/operations, up to five-year financials, debt/borrowings/charges/lenders, ratings, legal/IBC/default/negative developments, trade, buyers/suppliers, competitors, procurement/opportunity, credit safety, gaps and management takeaways.
4. Keep every material finding source-linked and dated where possible.
5. Apply evidence class: VERIFIED / DERIVED / ESTIMATED / PREDICTED / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN.
6. Keep commercial opportunity separate from credit/payment safety.
7. Apply `files/research-proof.md` before treating output as valid.
8. Persist/reload/export only through the existing application when performing a live system test.

Read `../../references/research-and-evidence.md` and `../../references/cost-and-reporting.md` when needed.

Never fill missing data to make charts look complete.
