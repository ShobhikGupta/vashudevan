# Research Methodology and Evidence Rules

## Order
Exact entity → identity/directors/ownership → business/operations → financial/debt/ratings → legal/default/negative → trade/buyers/suppliers → competitors/procurement/opportunity → source validation → evidence classification → final report.

## Core rules
- Exact legal entity first.
- Large company ≠ safe credit.
- High turnover ≠ liquidity.
- Profit ≠ cash.
- Registered charge ≠ current outstanding debt.
- No negative result ≠ proof of timely payment/no disputes.
- Company website is first-party evidence, not independent proof.
- Observed customs/trade data is coverage-limited.
- Opportunity is separate from credit/payment safety.
- Missing evidence remains UNKNOWN/PARTIAL.
- ESTIMATED/PREDICTED require explicit basis; never disguise them as VERIFIED.
- Every material finding should retain source/date/period where available.
- Conflicts remain visible; do not silently reconcile them.
- Graphs use real numeric points only.
