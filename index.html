ABOUT US

Vashudevan MetGlobal LLP is a leading name in the trade of non ferrous and ferrous metal scrap, headquartered in Surat, Gujarat, India. We have strong sourcing networks across India, Latin America and the United States, with regular trade activity in Asia, Europe, Canada and the Middle East. Our company provides complete solutions for commodity logistics, risk management, trade finance and market intelligence to support our partners around the world.

We operate across a wide network of trading hubs, including the USA, Canada, Mexico, South America, UK, Europe, Australia, New Zealand, India, China, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Korea, Taiwan, Pakistan, Africa, Middle East, Georgia and Mauritius.

Founded on principles of integrity and mutual success, we are known as aggressive and dependable buyers who are committed to shipping only quality material. Our main goal is to build and maintain strong relationships with suppliers, buyers and clients by understanding the needs of the physical commodities supply chain and consistently adding value at every stage.
