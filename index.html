Evidence standard

How the system treats information

The product is designed to stop a polished-looking company profile from being mistaken for genuine counterparty due diligence. Every important number carries an evidence state and missing data remains visible.

VERIFIED

Directly supported by a reliable primary or high-quality source.

DERIVED

Calculated from verified inputs using transparent maths.

ESTIMATED

Evidence-backed range with explicit formula and assumptions.

PREDICTED

Future scenario with low/base/high cases and assumptions.

UNKNOWN

Not sufficiently verifiable. Never filled with a guess.

Credit hard stop

High turnover ≠ liquidity. Profit ≠ cash. Active tax registration ≠ creditworthiness. Bank finance ≠ supplier payment quality. No court case found ≠ no payment dispute.
