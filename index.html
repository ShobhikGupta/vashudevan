ABOUT VMG

Built Around Reliable Global Supply

Connecting recyclable-metal supply with responsible international demand through disciplined coordination and long-term relationships.

Learn More About VMG →

Vashudevan MetGlobal LLP is a Surat, India–based company focused on the sourcing, supply and international movement of ferrous and non-ferrous recyclable metals. We work with buyers, suppliers, processors, yards and logistics partners across key global markets, supporting requirements from material review and commercial coordination through documentation, container loading and shipment planning.

Our approach is built on clear communication, dependable execution and long-term business relationships. Through a growing network across India, North America, Latin America, Europe, Asia, the Middle East, Africa and Oceania, VMG helps connect recyclable-metal supply with responsible global demand while maintaining focus on material quality, shipment discipline and commercial clarity.
