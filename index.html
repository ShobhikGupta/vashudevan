19. Could our money get stuck?

The live version checks current debt, operating cash, creditor ageing, supplier disputes, MSME delayed-payment complaints, recovery cases, cheque dishonour, insolvency, bank enforcement, auditor warnings and group-company stress. If recent evidence is missing, the result must say CREDIT SAFETY NOT SUFFICIENTLY VERIFIED.

26. Commercial opportunity

The opportunity engine is separate from the credit engine. A company can be commercially attractive while still requiring advance, LC, secured terms or a small controlled credit limit.

Research history

V1 will store researched companies, previous reports and later show what changed since the last review.

Methodology

Every important fact is classified as VERIFIED, DERIVED, ESTIMATED, PREDICTED or UNKNOWN. Registered bank charge is never treated as current debt. High turnover is never treated as proof of liquidity. No negative news is never treated as proof of good supplier payment behaviour.
