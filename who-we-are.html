WHO WE ARE

OUR HISTORY

Founded in 2019, Vashudevan MetGlobal LLP has grown into a truly global metals and commodities trading company. From the very beginning, our leadership team focused on building a trusted and internationally connected platform for buying and selling ferrous and non ferrous metal scrap.

Our culture is built on service, quality and efficiency. Through a disciplined approach to logistics, risk management and compliance, Vashudevan MetGlobal LLP has become a recognized name in the scrap metal trade, sourcing material across India, Latin America and the United States, and delivering to buyers across Asia, Europe, Africa, Canada, the Middle East and beyond.

Our strength lies in our ability to move material quickly and efficiently across global markets, adapting to changes in demand and supply. This is made possible by our strong relationships, a well-established network, and a highly experienced team with deep knowledge of established and emerging markets worldwide.

Today, the Vashudevan MetGlobal network spans multiple key trading hubs including the USA, Canada, Mexico, South America, UK, Europe, Australia, New Zealand, India, China, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Korea, Taiwan, Pakistan, Africa, the Middle East, Georgia and Mauritius, trading in more than 40 countries.

OUR CORE VALUES

Vashudevan Met Global LLP Strives For Reliability Of Service And Competitiveness In Our Proposals, In Turn Generating The Greatest Possible Results For Our Commercial Partners.

Our vision is to be a global player, both in terms of buying and selling, so we can provide maximum value and real-time market intelligence to our suppliers and buyers. OUR MISSION

Shaping a Responsible and Sustainable Future

Our mission is to be a catalyst for positive change in the global recycling industry, where sustainability and business objectives work hand in hand. Trading and logistics are at the core of what we do, and we play an essential role in moving scrap metal from regions where it is available to the regions where it is needed most — reliably, efficiently and responsibly.

Recycling metals significantly reduces the energy and resources needed compared to raw material production. For instance, recycling steel uses 60 percent less energy, while recycling aluminium saves an incredible 95 percent of the energy required to produce it from raw ore. Every container of scrap we move helps conserve energy and protect the environment.

At Vashudevan MetGlobal LLP, our commitment is clear, to create a better, greener future for the generations to come, one container at a time.
