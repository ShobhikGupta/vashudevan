Material Overview

Auto Scrap scrap enquiries with specification-first coordination

Automotive scrap can contain multiple metals and components, so clear descriptions matter. Buyers and suppliers should identify the component type, condition, approximate quantity, packing or loading method, origin and any separation or contamination expectations before a quotation is discussed.

Availability is not implied. Requirements are handled based on grade, specification, quantity, origin, destination, inspection needs and commercial terms.

What to include in an enquiry

  • Material category and grade/specification
  • Required or available quantity
  • Origin/loading location and destination
  • Packing, preparation and shipment readiness
  • Current photos or videos where useful
  • Inspection, acceptance and commercial requirements

Grades & Search Terms

Common auto scrap categories in VMG enquiries

These terms help buyers and suppliers identify a requirement. The agreed transaction specification always controls final acceptance.

AC compressor scrap

State whether units are drained or otherwise prepared as required by the transaction, plus quantity, origin, packing and condition.

Alternators and starters

Motor and alternator components can contain ferrous, aluminium and copper-bearing fractions. Buyers should specify the accepted preparation and mix.

Engines and transmissions

State body/material type, condition, completeness and any fluids or attachments relevant to acceptance.

Automotive components

Current VMG catalogue also lists ECMs, armatures, field coils, rotors, solenoids and stators for appropriate enquiries.
