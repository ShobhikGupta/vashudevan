Material Overview

Aluminium scrap enquiries with specification-first coordination

Aluminium (also spelled aluminum) scrap is traded in many forms, alloys and recovery categories. Clear segregation and an agreed specification are important because alloy series, attachments, coatings, contamination and material form can affect suitability for a buyer. VMG coordinates requirements and material offers against the specific transaction rather than presenting permanent inventory.

Availability is not implied. Requirements are handled based on grade, specification, quantity, origin, destination, inspection needs and commercial terms.

What to include in an enquiry

  • Material category and grade/specification
  • Required or available quantity
  • Origin/loading location and destination
  • Packing, preparation and shipment readiness
  • Current photos or videos where useful
  • Inspection, acceptance and commercial requirements

Grades & Search Terms

Common aluminium categories in VMG enquiries

These terms help buyers and suppliers identify a requirement. The agreed transaction specification always controls final acceptance.

6061 aluminium scrap

6061 enquiries may include truck wheels, sheets, plates or clips. Buyers should state alloy, form, attachments, quantity and acceptance criteria.

6063 aluminium scrap

6063 is commonly associated with extrusion applications. Requirements should state whether material is clean extrusion, irony extrusion or another agreed form.

What is UBC / used beverage can scrap?

UBC refers to used beverage can scrap. Offers should state bale or packing details, approximate quantity, origin, condition and any agreed contamination or moisture limits.

What is Taint / Tabor aluminium scrap?

Taint/Tabor is a widely used aluminium scrap trade term. The current buyer/seller specification should govern the exact material definition and acceptance basis.

What is Tense aluminium scrap?

Tense is used for cast aluminium scrap in industry terminology. Final grade, contamination and recovery expectations should be agreed for the transaction.

Other aluminium categories

VMG’s current catalogue also lists radiators, wheels, pistons, litho sheets, ACSR, EC wire and other aluminium-bearing categories for enquiry.
