Material Overview

Ferrous scrap enquiries with specification-first coordination

Ferrous scrap requirements vary by grade, dimensions, density, preparation and contamination limits. Buyers should state the accepted specification and destination; suppliers should provide material form, quantity, loading details, origin and current visual evidence where useful.

Availability is not implied. Requirements are handled based on grade, specification, quantity, origin, destination, inspection needs and commercial terms.

What to include in an enquiry

  • Material category and grade/specification
  • Required or available quantity
  • Origin/loading location and destination
  • Packing, preparation and shipment readiness
  • Current photos or videos where useful
  • Inspection, acceptance and commercial requirements

Grades & Search Terms

Common ferrous categories in VMG enquiries

These terms help buyers and suppliers identify a requirement. The agreed transaction specification always controls final acceptance.

HMS 1 & 2

Heavy melting scrap is listed in VMG’s current ferrous catalogue. The exact grade mix, dimensions and acceptance basis should be agreed for each transaction.

Rail and re-rolling scrap

Rail scrap and re-rolling scrap are represented as separate catalogue categories.

PNS and LMS bales

PNS and LMS bale enquiries should specify preparation, dimensions or bale details where relevant.

Other ferrous categories

Current catalogue also lists cast rotors and drums, H13 solids, moon-cut pipes and steel turnings.
