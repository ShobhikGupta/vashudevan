Material Overview

Copper scrap enquiries with specification-first coordination

Copper scrap value and suitability depend on the agreed grade, form, cleanliness and composition. Industry code names are useful shorthand, but the current specification and contract terms should define the accepted material for each transaction.

Availability is not implied. Requirements are handled based on grade, specification, quantity, origin, destination, inspection needs and commercial terms.

What to include in an enquiry

  • Material category and grade/specification
  • Required or available quantity
  • Origin/loading location and destination
  • Packing, preparation and shipment readiness
  • Current photos or videos where useful
  • Inspection, acceptance and commercial requirements

Grades & Search Terms

Common copper categories in VMG enquiries

These terms help buyers and suppliers identify a requirement. The agreed transaction specification always controls final acceptance.

What are Berry / Candy copper grades?

ReMA uses Berry and Candy as copper scrap specification code names. VMG catalogue groups #1 copper under Berry/Candy; final acceptance follows the agreed current specification.

What are Birch / Cliff copper grades?

ReMA uses Birch and Cliff for #2 copper wire and solids/tubing categories. VMG catalogue groups #2 copper under Birch/Cliff.

Barley

VMG catalogue lists bare bright wire under the Barley trade term. Buyers and suppliers should confirm the current specification rather than relying on a code name alone.

Specification first

Include grade, condition, quantity, origin, destination, photos where useful, and inspection/acceptance criteria in the enquiry.
