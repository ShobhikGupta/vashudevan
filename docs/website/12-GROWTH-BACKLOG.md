## First 90 days
**Month 1:** benchmark Search Console/GA4, make enquiry storage reliable, fix crawl coverage and duplication.  
**Month 2:** ship 3 owner-reviewed cornerstone category pages and 4–6 useful grade pages, with strong internal linking.  
**Month 3:** publish technical buyer/supplier guides, assess qualified leads and organic impressions, improve pages based on real query data.

## How to judge success
- Organic qualified enquiries / organic engaged sessions.
- Nonbranded impressions and clicks by grade/category.
- Form success rate confirmed in destination.
- Lead-to-reply elapsed time.
- Technical health (CWV, broken links, missing titles and indexed duplicate URLs).

**Caution:** this plan can improve discoverability and lead conversion opportunities; neither growth nor rankings are guaranteed.
