Documentation Support

Essential Trade Documentation

A concise reference to common documents, inspection basics, commercial terms, and counterparty verification used in international recyclable-metal trade.

01

Import / Export Documents

Supporting records may include a Commercial Invoice, Packing List or Commercial Invoice cum Packing List, Bill of Lading or Airway Bill, and Bill of Entry or Shipping Bill as applicable.

02

PSI / Inspection Basics

Inspection and certification requirements may vary by material, transaction, buyer requirement, origin and destination. Where applicable, third-party inspection coordination may be discussed.

03

Incoterms Basics

Incoterms® are ICC trade terms used to clarify delivery responsibilities, costs and risks between seller and buyer. They do not replace the complete contract.

04

KYC & Counterparty Verification

Company registration details, authorised contacts, business address and relevant verification information may be requested depending on the transaction and circumstances.

Documentation and verification requirements may vary by material, origin, destination, counterparty and transaction terms.
