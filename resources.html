Our Process

How We Move Recyclable Metals Globally

A structured process helps reduce confusion, improve communication, and support dependable recyclable-metal movement across global markets.

Enquiry Received

We review the buyer requirement, supplier offer, partnership enquiry, or documentation request.

Material Grade & Quantity Reviewed

Material category, grade, quantity, origin, destination, quality expectations, and shipment readiness are checked.

Supplier / Buyer Coordination

We coordinate with verified networks to align availability, requirements, communication, and next steps.

Pricing & Terms Discussion

Commercial terms, shipment basis, inspection needs, payment structure, and timeline are discussed as per the enquiry.

Documentation Support

Relevant commercial, shipment, inspection, and compliance-related documentation is coordinated where applicable.

Container Loading & Shipment Coordination

For container-based movement, loading information, container details, shipment planning, and logistics coordination are managed with discipline.

Ongoing Trade Communication

We maintain clear communication across the transaction lifecycle to support reliability, transparency, and long-term business relationships.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for buyers, suppliers, and business partners exploring recyclable-metal opportunities with Vashudevan MetGlobal LLP.

What materials does Vashudevan MetGlobal LLP support?
We support ferrous and non-ferrous recyclable-metal categories including aluminium, copper, brass, ferrous scrap, stainless steel, lead, zinc, auto scrap, shredder scrap, and copper-bearing materials.
Can international suppliers contact VMG?
Yes. International suppliers can contact VMG with material details, quantity, origin, loading location, photos/videos, inspection availability, and shipment readiness.
Can buyers send specific material requirements?
Yes. Buyers can send their required material category, grade/specification, quantity, destination port or location, inspection preference, shipment timeline, and commercial terms.
Are the Market page prices final quotations?
No. Market page prices are indicative market references only. Physical scrap pricing can differ by grade, recovery, quality, location, freight, duties, documentation, and market conditions.
Is third-party inspection available?
Third-party inspection such as SGS/BV or other inspection support may be arranged where applicable, depending on final commercial terms, material type, location, and buyer/supplier requirements.
What documents are usually required for import/export?
Common documents may include commercial invoice, packing list, contract or purchase order, bill of lading, certificate of origin where applicable, inspection certificate where applicable, and other shipment-specific documents.
How can I submit a material offer?
Use the Submit Material Offer form and share material grade, quantity, origin, loading location, photos/videos, target price, inspection availability, and contact details.
How can I request a bulk quotation?
Use the Send Buying Requirement or Bulk Quotation form and include the required grade/specification, quantity, destination, shipment terms, inspection requirement, and expected timeline.
Where is Vashudevan MetGlobal LLP based?
Vashudevan MetGlobal LLP is headquartered in Surat, Gujarat, India, and supports recyclable-metal sourcing, supply, documentation, and shipment coordination across international markets.
How can I download the company profile?
You can download the Vashudevan MetGlobal LLP company profile from the Resources page, homepage company profile section, footer, or Need Help menu.
