Documentation Support

Essential Trade Documentation

A concise reference to common documents, inspection basics, commercial terms, and KYC requirements used in international recyclable-metal trade.

01

Import / Export Documents

Common documents may include commercial invoice, packing list, PO/contract, bill of lading, certificate of origin, inspection certificate where applicable, and shipment-specific documents.

02

PSIC / Inspection Basics

Inspection and certification requirements may vary by material, origin, destination, loading location, inspection terms, and current regulations.

03

Incoterms Basics

FOB, CFR, CIF, EXW, and related trade terms help define responsibility, cost, and risk between buyer and seller before shipment.

04

KYC Document Requirements

Serious business enquiries may require company registration details, tax/import-export registrations where applicable, authorised contacts, business address, and commercially relevant verification details.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for buyers, suppliers, and business partners working with VMG.

What materials does Vashudevan MetGlobal LLP support?
We support ferrous and non-ferrous recyclable-metal categories including aluminium, copper, brass, ferrous scrap, stainless steel, lead, zinc, auto scrap, shredder scrap, and copper-bearing materials.
Can buyers send specific material requirements?
Yes. Buyers can share required material category, grade/specification, quantity, destination port or location, inspection preference, shipment timeline, and commercial terms.
Can suppliers submit material offers?
Yes. Suppliers can submit material grade, origin, available quantity, loading location, photos/videos, inspection availability, shipment readiness, and contact details.
Are Market page prices final quotations?
No. Market page prices are indicative market references only. Physical recyclable-metal pricing can differ by grade, recovery, quality, location, freight, duties, documentation, and market conditions.
Is third-party inspection available?
Third-party inspection such as SGS/BV or other inspection support may be arranged where applicable, depending on commercial terms, material type, location, and buyer/supplier requirements.
What documents are usually required for import/export?
Common documents may include commercial invoice, packing list, contract or purchase order, bill of lading, certificate of origin where applicable, inspection certificate where applicable, and other shipment-specific documents.
How can I submit a material offer?
Use the supplier resource tab or contact form and share grade, quantity, origin, loading location, photos/videos, target price, inspection availability, and shipment readiness.
How can I download the company profile?
Use the dedicated Company Profile section near the top of this Resources page to preview or download the current VMG PDF.
