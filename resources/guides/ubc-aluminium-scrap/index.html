What Is UBC Aluminium Scrap?

UBC means used beverage can scrap. It is a common aluminium recycling stream, but transaction quality still depends on the agreed condition, packing, contamination, moisture and other buyer/seller requirements.

What UBC means

UBC is an industry abbreviation for used beverage cans collected for recycling. VMG’s current aluminium catalogue lists Used Beverage Cans (TALDON) (UBC).

What buyers should specify

A buyer should state required quantity, destination, bale or packing expectations, acceptable condition, contamination/moisture basis, inspection requirements and commercial terms.

What suppliers should provide

A supplier should state available quantity, origin/loading location, packing, current condition, recent photos or videos, readiness and any available quality information.

Why the exact specification matters

Trade names help identify a material stream, but a contract should define the acceptance basis. Do not assume every bale or shipment described as UBC has identical characteristics.
